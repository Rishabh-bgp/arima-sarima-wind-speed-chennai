About the design. 坎 (Kǎn) is water: flowing and returning to its level. It fits coastal Chennai and ARIMA's mean-reverting forecasts. The red seal (印) carries the initials RA. The taiji and the eight Bagua trigrams stand for balance and change. The vertical motto 上善若水 means "The highest good is like water".
